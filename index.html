Client-side building simulation

EnergyPlus in the browser

The U.S. Department of Energy whole-building engine, compiled to WebAssembly and running inside this tab. Out of the box it solves a single free-running zone against Denver design conditions, or against any of 17,292 weather stations and the design conditions that come with them. The model, the solver and the results never leave your machine — there is no simulation server behind this page.
