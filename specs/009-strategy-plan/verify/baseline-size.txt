Pre-feature transfer baseline for SC-015.

Taken on the feature branch's parent commit, 82d098d, with `npm run build` in
a clean worktree of that commit, so the one-line fix to `src/pull.js` that this
branch carries ahead of the feature is not counted in either figure. Every
figure is of `dist/` **excluding** the three staged directories `energyplus/`,
`schemas/` and `weather/`: those are engine assets, identical before and after,
and at about 50 MB they would swamp a 60 KB delta in the noise of their own
brotli settings.

Brotli is applied the way `scripts/deploy.mjs` applies it (quality 9 above
4 MiB, quality 11 below), because SC-015 is stated as transfer on a cold visit
and the deploy script's own settings decide that figure, not the raw byte count
vite writes. The measuring script is the one the 006 baseline used: walk
`dist/`, skip the three staged directories, compress each file with those
settings, and sum.

                                          raw     brotli
assets/index-*.js                      605681     175403
index.html                             246276      50156
----------------------------------------------------------
TOTAL                                  851957     225559

SC-015's ceiling is 60 KB of **added transfer**, so the figure this feature
must come in under is a post-feature brotli total of 225559 + 61440 =
**286999 bytes**.

Re-measure with the same script rather than with `ls`: `index.html` carries the
whole stylesheet inline, so a style-only change moves a number that a
JavaScript-only measurement would miss entirely.

After the feature, same script, same brotli settings:

                                          raw     brotli
assets/index-*.js                      683754     198603
index.html                             259761      52015
----------------------------------------------------------
TOTAL                                  943515     250618

**Added transfer: 25,059 bytes** against SC-015's ceiling of 61,440, close to
research.md section 15's estimate of about 20 KB. Of it, about 1.5 KB is the
Joe and Kuo direction table for 128 dimensions, carried past the 85 faces the
desk has today so that appending a control never touches it. No asset, font or
binary was added, and no new output variable is requested, so the engine's own
payload is untouched.
