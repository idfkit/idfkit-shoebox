Pre-feature transfer baseline for SC-012.

Taken on the feature branch's parent commit, 7cc252a, with
`npm run build`. Every figure is of `dist/` **excluding** the three staged
directories `energyplus/`, `schemas/` and `weather/`: those are engine assets,
not this feature's code, they are identical before and after, and at ~50 MB they
would swamp a 60 KB delta in the noise of their own brotli settings.

Brotli is applied here the way `scripts/deploy.mjs` applies it — quality 9 above
4 MiB and quality 11 below — because SC-012 is stated as transfer on a cold
visit and the deploy script's own settings are what decide that figure, not the
raw byte count vite writes.

                                          raw     brotli
assets/index-*.js                      506759     145468
index.html                             205345      42343
----------------------------------------------------------
TOTAL                                  712104     187811

SC-012's ceiling is 60 KB of **added transfer**, so the figure this feature must
come in under is a post-feature brotli total of 187811 + 61440 = **249251 bytes**.

Re-measure with the same script rather than with `ls`: `index.html` carries the
whole stylesheet inline, so a style-only change moves a number that a
JavaScript-only measurement would miss entirely.
